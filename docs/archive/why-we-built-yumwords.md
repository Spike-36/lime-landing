# Why We Built YumWords

Archived on 21 October 2026 from `src/components/JourneySection.tsx`, an
unused section of the old multi-product YumWords homepage. This is the last
(uncommitted) wording of that section, kept for possible reuse on a future
homepage or About page. The component itself was removed.

---

YumWords grew out of a long-standing interest in food, language and cultural discovery — both at home and while travelling.

Over the years, we found ourselves repeatedly facing the same frustration: understanding unfamiliar dishes, ingredients and menus often meant switching between translation apps, reviews, blog posts and scattered online searches — sometimes with unreliable Wi-Fi or limited mobile data making the experience even more frustrating.

We originally created YumWords for ourselves during our own travels as a way to make food discovery simpler, clearer and more enjoyable. We wanted a single place that combined language, cultural context and practical guidance without the usual friction and confusion.

Today, the apps continue to evolve across different cuisines and experiences — from Asian street food to French cooking and European cheese.
